How it works

1. Load the video. 2. Check that the circles sit on the wells and the dashed box covers the temperature text. 3. Press Analyse. 4. Review each well and correct any call with one click.

A fly counts as knocked down from the last time its body moved more than half a body length; leg kicking afterwards is ignored. The layout you set is remembered in this browser.

Check the layout

Drag the two filled dots onto the centres of the first and last wells (A1 and D6 on your plate); the grid follows, rotation included. Drag inside any well to move the whole grid. Click a well to include or exclude it. Drag the dashed box over the temperature text (corner handle resizes it).

Knockdown temperature by group (dots = flies, diamond = mean ± SE)

Linear model on the knockdown temperatures (one value per fly): the effect of each factor, their interaction, and the differences along the axis. Flies still standing at the end are left out; the curves below include them.

Flies still standing, by group

Log-rank test: compares whole curves, counting flies still standing at the end as censored.

Activity

Distance walked per bin, from positions taken once per second; steps under 0.2 body lengths per second (twitching, tracking jitter) are not counted.

Flies still standing: mean ± SE against temperature

Each point averages the flies of a group that are still standing, over a sliding window one bin wide (moved a quarter bin at a time). The band is ± SE; hover a point for the number of flies. Flies without a knockdown call are left out.

Summarises each fly by its activity level and how fast it changes with temperature, then compares groups on those per-fly values.

How often flies walk

Share of seconds in which a standing fly walked (at least 0.2 body lengths per second), same sliding window. This is the “how often” part of activity.

Same approach: per-fly level and change per °C of the time spent walking.

Speed while walking

Mean speed over the seconds in which the fly walked, in body lengths per second (windows with fewer than 3 walking seconds are left out). This is the “how fast” part of activity.

Same approach: per-fly level and change per °C of the walking speed.

Activity before knockdown

Each fly lined up on its own knockdown (0 = knockdown); activity over the preceding 30 s, mean ± SE per group. Shows how activity changes in the run-up to collapse, whatever the temperature at which it happens.

Compares the activity shortly before collapse, and how sharply it falls in the last 2 min, between groups.

One value per fly

Activity is counted only while the fly is standing. Dots = flies, diamond = mean ± SE.

Linear model on the plotted value (one per fly).
